Frequently asked questions

Questions from wholesalers. Honest answers.

Why specialise in wholesale?

Wholesalers run into the same bottlenecks: finding buyers, following up quotes, processing orders. Because we focus on exactly that, you don't need to explain how your market works.

We're not a wholesaler. Can you still help us?

Yes. Wholesale is our speciality, but the same leaks show up at manufacturers, importers, installers and other B2B companies that work with enquiries, quotes and regular customers. Feel free to take the profit scan or book a call. If it's not a fit, we'll tell you honestly.

What if it doesn't work for our business?

That's why you first see a working demo with your processes, before you invest in the build. And because we earn from your growth, we have just as much at stake in making it work.

What does it cost?

The profit scan is free. For the build we agree clear costs up front. After that, we earn from what it delivers.

Do we need to be doing anything with AI already?

No. We start with your processes, not the technology. Your team doesn't need any AI knowledge.

Does it work with our ERP or webshop?

That's the starting point. We build on the systems you already use. In the profit scan we check which integrations are possible.

What if AI isn't the best solution?

Then we'll tell you. Sometimes a simple automation or faster follow-up delivers more.

Am I tied to anything?

No. The profit scan and build plan are no-strings. We only start building after your yes.
